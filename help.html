Help

Answers about registering and joining clubs.

▶ Who can register a club?

Any currently enrolled CSUN student can start a recognized student organization.

▶ What do I need before I start?

At least 3 officers and a faculty advisor.

▶ What happens after I submit?

The MIC staff reviews your constitution and paperwork within 3-5 business days.

▶ Can I edit my club after it is approved?

Yes, through your club dashboard.

▶ How do I join a club?

Click 'View club' on any listing and select Join club.

▶ I cannot log in. What should I do?

Ensure you are using your active MyCSUN credentials or contact IT.
